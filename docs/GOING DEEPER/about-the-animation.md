## The Animation Eras

### 🖊️ Classic Era (East Blue through Alabasta)

Episodes 1–130

Thick lines, flat colors, simple backgrounds.
Charming and nostalgic. The animation quality reflects
a young crew starting their journey. The older visual
style holds up well because the storytelling carries
every scene.

### ⚔️ Golden Era (Skypiea through Enies Lobby)

Episodes 131–325

The animation quality begins finding its footing.
The Enies Lobby arc in particular features some
of the most emotionally powerful sequences
in the entire series. Toei invested heavily here.

### 🌊 Transition Era (Post Timeskip through Dressrosa)

Episodes 517–746

New character designs after the timeskip.
Slimmer lines, more textured backgrounds.
Animation quality varies between episodes,
but the strongest moments reach genuine brilliance.

### 🔥 Modern Era (Whole Cake Island through Wano)

Episodes 783–1085

A dramatic animation quality upgrade. Wano in particular
features cinematic direction, sakuga-packed
fight scenes, Japanese ink-inspired aesthetics,
and sequences that rival theatrical releases.

### ✨ Final Saga (Egghead onward)

Episodes 1086–present

The highest animation quality in the show's history.
Toei adopted a seasonal production structure,
releasing roughly 26 episodes per year instead
of the previous 40–50. Each episode now receives
more time and resources for storyboarding,
key animation, and compositing. The result
looks like a different show in the best way.

## What Is Sakuga?

**Sakuga** refers to moments in anime where the
animation quality noticeably elevates. Key animators
receive the freedom to showcase their craft on
important scenes. People across the anime community
consider One Piece sakuga moments legendary.

## The Remake

**WIT Studio** (Attack on Titan, Spy x Family)
is producing **The One Piece** for Netflix.
Starting from East Blue with modern animation.
Currently in production as of 2026.

## A Note On Toei

Toei produces One Piece on a weekly schedule
while simultaneously producing other major series.
The fact that they have maintained this show
for 25 years at any quality level is remarkable.
The animation quality does not always hit perfection.
The story always does. 🏴‍☠️
