<Accordion title="The Basics">
  **What even is One Piece?**

  A manga and anime series by Eiichiro Oda about
  a boy made of rubber who wants to be
  <Glossary>pirate king</Glossary>.

  That is the simple version.

  The real version involves 28 years of
  storytelling, 500 million manga copies,
  a world so detailed it has its own
  academic courses, and a fandom that
  will not stop crying.

  ***

  **Who created it?**

  **Eiichiro Oda.** One person. Since 1997.
  Still going. Sleeping 3 hours a night.
  See our [Meet Eiichiro Oda](/docs/meet-eiichiro-oda)
  page for the full story.

  He does not take himself seriously.
  The man puts easter eggs about ramen
  in the background of his panels.
  We love him.

  ***

  **Is it really worth starting in 2026?**

  Yes. Unequivocally.

  The story is approaching its conclusion
  after 28 years. The anime is producing
  its best work in history. The community
  is more active than ever.

  There has never been a better time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Episode Count">
  **1155 episodes. Are you serious.**

  Yes. And people who finish it wish
  there were more.

  ***

  **How long will it actually take?**

  At 24 minutes per episode:

  * At 2 episodes per day = about 20 months
  * At 5 episodes per day = about 8 months
  * At 10 episodes per day = about 4 months

  Most people find themselves watching
  more than planned. Every time.

  ***

  **Can I skip episodes?**

  Yes. See our
  [Episodes To Skip](/docs/episodes-to-skip)
  guide for the full breakdown.

  You can skip <Glossary>filler</Glossary> episodes.
  Some <Glossary>filler</Glossary> arcs are
  genuinely beloved though. G-8 specifically
  is worth considering.

  ***

  **When does it get good?**

  It is good from Episode 1.

  It gets GREAT around Episode 31.

  It becomes something else entirely
  around Episode 264.

  After that it never stops getting better.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Show">
  **Is the early animation really that bad?**

  The early animation is older.
  That is different from bad.

  The storytelling is excellent from
  the very beginning. By Wano you will
  be watching some of the greatest
  animation Toei has ever produced.

  Trust the journey.

  ***

  **Sub or dub?**

  Both are genuinely great.

  **Sub** — The Japanese cast has voiced
  these characters for 25 years.
  Many fans consider it definitive.

  **Dub** — Funimation's English cast
  brings real heart to every role.

  Watch in whatever language feels natural.
  Neither choice is wrong.

  ***

  **Are the movies worth watching?**

  Several of them yes. See our
  [Movie Guide](/docs/movie-guide)
  for the full breakdown.

  Strong World, Film Z, Film Gold,
  Stampede, and Film Red are all
  worth your time.

  Film Red is essential if you are
  watching the Final Saga.

  ***

  **Is the manga ahead of the anime?**

  Yes. The manga is currently in the
  <Glossary>Elbaf</Glossary> arc.

  The anime's Elbaf arc premieres
  in April 2026.

  ***

  **Will I cry?**

  Yes.

  Oda was asked once if he wanted
  readers to cry at a certain scene.

  He said he wanted them to cry
  so hard they could not breathe.

  He succeeded. Multiple times.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Lore">
  **What are<Glossary>devil fruit</Glossary>s exactly?**

  Mysterious fruits found throughout the world.
  Each one grants a unique power to whoever eats it.

  The cost: the eater can never swim again.

  In a world made almost entirely of ocean
  this is a significant trade off.

  There are three types:
  <Glossary>Paramecia</Glossary>,
  <Glossary>Zoan</Glossary>, and
  <Glossary>Logia</Glossary>.
  Each works differently.

  ***

  **What is<Glossary>Haki</Glossary>?**

  A spiritual energy that exists in all
  living things. Most people never awaken it.

  Those who do gain abilities that go
  beyond any <Glossary>devil fruit</Glossary>.

  The story introduces it gradually
  as you progress. Do not worry about
  understanding it right away.

  ***

  **What is the<Glossary>One Piece</Glossary> actually?**

  Nobody knows.

  Including us.

  Including most characters in the story.

  Gol D. Roger found it.
  He laughed when he did.

  That is all anyone knows for certain. 🏴‍☠️

  ***

  **What is the Void Century?**

  A 100 year period of history that the
  <Glossary>world government</Glossary>
  has erased from all records.

  The truth is written on
  <Glossary>poneglyphs</Glossary>
  scattered across the world.

  Only one person alive can read them.

  This mystery runs through the entire series.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Beyond The Anime">
  **What is the difference between
  the manga and the anime?**

  The **manga** is Oda's original work.
  Black and white panels. Released weekly.
  Currently ahead of the anime.

  The **anime** is Toei Animation's
  adaptation. Color, voice acting, music.
  Follows the manga closely with some
  additional scenes and <Glossary>filler</Glossary>.

  Both tell the same story.
  The manga is faster.
  The anime is more immersive.

  ***

  **Is there a card game?**

  Yes! The **One Piece Card Game** launched
  in 2022 and became one of the fastest
  growing trading card games in the world.

  It features characters from across
  the entire series and is genuinely
  fun whether you play competitively
  or just collect.

  A great way to get more invested in
  the characters before or while watching.

  ***

  **Where can I read the manga?**

  Officially on **Viz Media** and
  **Manga Plus** — both have the
  first three and latest three chapters
  free at all times.

  A Shonen Jump subscription on Viz
  gets you access to everything for
  about $3 a month. Genuinely worth it.

  ***

  **Where can I watch the anime?**

  * **Crunchyroll** — Sub and dub,
    simulcast for new episodes
  * **Netflix** — Sub and dub,
    currently up to Egghead Arc
  * **Hulu** — Sub only

  ***

  **What is Jump Festa?**

  An annual Japanese convention celebrating
  Weekly Shonen Jump manga and anime.
  One Piece regularly makes major announcements
  there including new arcs, movies, and projects.

  It is essentially Christmas for One Piece fans.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The Community">
  **Why are One Piece fans so intense?**

  Because the show earns it.

  Oda has told one continuous story
  for 28 years. Plot threads pay off
  hundreds of episodes after they are
  planted. Characters grow in ways
  that make you genuinely care.

  The fandom's dedication reflects the
  dedication Oda puts into every chapter.

  You will understand when you get there.

  ***

  **What is a<Glossary>nakama</Glossary>?**

  The Japanese word for crewmate
  or companion.

  In One Piece it means something deeper.
  Not just friends. Family chosen through
  shared struggle and unwavering loyalty.

  By the end of this show you will
  understand exactly what it means.
</Accordion>
