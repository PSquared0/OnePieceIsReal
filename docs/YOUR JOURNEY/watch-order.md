# Watch Order 🗺️

One Piece has a straightforward watch order with
one exception: the movies.

The main series watches in episode order from
Episode 1 to Episode 1155. No jumping around needed.

The movies are where it gets interesting.

***

## The Main Series

Watch episodes in order.

**Start:** Episode 1
**Current end:** Episode 1155
**Status:** Ongoing — new episodes air in 2026

Use our **[Episodes To Skip](/docs/episodes-to-skip)**
guide alongside this to identify
<Glossary>filler</Glossary> episodes.

***

## The Movies

Most One Piece movies are <Glossary>non-canon</Glossary>.
They exist outside the main timeline and do not
affect the main story. Characters will not reference
them. Plot points will not carry over.

Think of them as "What if" adventures featuring
your favorite crew at their best.

One exception: **Film Red** contains
<Glossary>canon</Glossary>-adjacent lore that
connects directly to the Final Saga.

They are absolutely worth watching. Just know
going in where they fit.

***

## 🎬 Movie Watch Order

**One Piece: The Movie (2000)**
Watch after Episode 18

**Clockwork Island Adventure (2001)**
Watch after Episode 53

**Chopper's Kingdom on the Island
of Strange Animals (2002)**
Watch after Episode 130

**Dead End Adventure (2003)**
Watch after Episode 195

**The Cursed Holy Sword (2004)**
Watch after Episode 195

**Baron Omatsuri and the Secret Island (2005)**
Watch after Episode 195

<Callout icon="🎬" theme="default">
  Directed by Mamoru Hosoda — the same
  creative force behind Wolf Children and
  The Girl Who Leapt Through Time.
  Darker than most One Piece movies.
  Worth watching for the craft alone.
</Callout>

**The Giant Mechanical Soldier
of Karakuri Castle (2006)**
Watch after Episode 195

**The Desert Princess and the Pirates:
Adventures in Alabasta (2007)**
Watch after Episode 130

<Callout icon="📖" theme="default">
  A retelling of the Alabasta arc.
  Watch the arc first then this as a companion.
</Callout>

**Episode of Chopper Plus:
Bloom in the Winter, Miracle Sakura (2008)**
Watch after Episode 325

<Callout icon="📖" theme="default">
  A retelling of the Drum Island arc.
</Callout>

**Strong World (2009)** ⭐
Watch after Episode 425

<Callout icon="✍️" theme="default">
  The first movie with direct Oda involvement.
  He wrote the story himself.
  Considered essential by many fans.
</Callout>

**3D2Y (2014 TV Special)**
Watch after Episode 516

<Callout icon="📺" theme="default">
  Not a movie but fits here chronologically.
  Covers Luffy's two year training period
  after the <Glossary>timeskip</Glossary>.
</Callout>

**Film Z (2012)** ⭐
Watch after Episode 590

**Film Gold (2016)** ⭐
Watch after Episode 750

**Stampede (2019)** ⭐
Watch after Episode 894

<Callout icon="🎉" theme="default">
  Fan service masterpiece.
  Features almost every major character
  in the series history including members
  of the <Glossary>straw hat pirates</Glossary>.
</Callout>

**Film Red (2022)** ⭐⭐
Watch after Episode 1029

<Callout icon="🚨" theme="default">
  <Glossary>canon</Glossary>-adjacent.
  Contains significant lore reveals that
  connect to the Final Saga.
  One of the best animated pieces of
  One Piece media ever made.
  Do not skip this one.
</Callout>

## A Note On The Movies

No movie is required to follow the main story.
But Strong World, Film Z, Film Gold, Stampede,
and Film Red genuinely add to the experience.

Film Red hits differently if you have been
watching since the beginning.

That is all we will say. 🏴‍☠️
