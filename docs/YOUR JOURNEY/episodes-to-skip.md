# Episodes To Skip 🏴‍☠️

One Piece has 1155 episodes. Not all of them
are created equal.

This guide breaks down exactly which episodes
are <Glossary>manga</Glossary> <Glossary>canon</Glossary>,
which are <Glossary>filler</Glossary>, and which
are mixed so you can watch efficiently without
missing anything that matters.

## What Do These Categories Mean?

**<Glossary>Manga</Glossary> <Glossary>Canon</Glossary>**
Directly adapted from Oda's manga.
Essential viewing. Never skip these.

**Anime <Glossary>Canon</Glossary>**
Created by Toei but fits the story naturally.
Generally worth watching.

**Mixed <Glossary>Canon</Glossary>/<Glossary>Filler</Glossary>**
Part canon, part original content.
Use your judgment. Most are fine to watch.

**<Glossary>Filler</Glossary>**
Completely original Toei content with no impact
on the main story. Safe to skip.

***

<Callout icon="💡" theme="default">
  ### **Pro tip:** If you are short on time,

  watch Manga <Glossary>Canon</Glossary> episodes only.
  You will not miss a single plot point.
</Callout>
