# Movie Guide 🎬

One Piece has 15 theatrical films plus several
TV specials. None are required to follow the
main story, but several are genuinely great.

This guide tells you where each film fits in
the watch order and which ones are worth
prioritizing.

***

## Before You Watch

All One Piece movies are <Glossary>non-canon</Glossary>
with one exception.

<Glossary>Non-canon</Glossary> means the events
never happened in the main story. Characters will
not reference them. Plot points will not carry over.

Think of them as "What if" adventures featuring
your favorite crew at their best.

The one exception: **Film Red** contains
<Glossary>canon</Glossary>-adjacent lore that
connects directly to the Final Saga. Do not
skip this one.

***

## Our Top Picks

If you only watch a few movies pick these:

1. **Strong World** — Oda wrote it himself
2. **Film Z** — emotionally hits hard
3. **Film Gold** — visually stunning
4. **Stampede** — pure fan service joy
5. **Film Red** — essential for Final Saga viewers

***

## A Note On The Early Films

The first seven movies are fun but not essential.
Watch them if you enjoy spending time with the
crew in low stakes adventures. Skip them if you
want to focus on the main story.

No judgment either way. 🏴‍☠️
