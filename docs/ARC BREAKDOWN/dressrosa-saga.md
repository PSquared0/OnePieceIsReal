The <Glossary>Dressrosa Saga</Glossary> is the
longest arc in One Piece history.

It is also one of the most beloved.

At over 100 episodes it tests your patience
in the best possible way. Every episode adds
something. Every character matters. Every
thread Oda planted in Punk Hazard pays off here.

By the end of Dressrosa you will understand
why people call One Piece the greatest
manga ever written.

## What Happens Here

No spoilers — here is what matters:

A kingdom lives under the rule of someone
who should not be there. The people have
forgotten what was taken from them.

The <Glossary>straw hat pirates</Glossary>
arrive and start pulling on threads.

The whole thing unravels beautifully.

## Key Episodes

**Episode 629** — Dressrosa begins.
The kingdom looks perfect.
Something is very wrong.

**Episode 651** — The tournament begins.
Some of the best fight choreography
in the series starts here.

**Episode 700** — The midpoint.
Everything accelerates from here.

**Episode 726** — The climax begins.
Oda pulls together every thread
he has been weaving for years.

**Episode 746** — The conclusion.
One of the most satisfying arc endings
in One Piece history.

<Callout icon="🚨" theme="default">
  Dressrosa is long. Some episodes move
  slowly. Push through.

  The payoff is one of the greatest moments
  in the entire series.

  Do not skip ahead. Do not look anything up.
</Callout>

adapt nearly everything faithfully.

## Featured Music

**Wake Up** — The opening theme
that carries most of Dressrosa.
Builds perfectly with the arc's
escalating intensity.

**Hard Knock Days** — Arrives in
the second half. More emotional
and reflective than Wake Up.

**Shiawase** — The ending theme.
Quietly beautiful after intense episodes.
