The <Glossary>Water 7 Saga</Glossary> is where
One Piece earns everything it has been building
toward since Episode 1.

This saga contains two of the most celebrated
arcs in the entire series. It will make you
laugh, devastate you, and leave you completely
unable to stop watching.

New viewers often cite this saga as the moment
they became a One Piece fan for life.

## What Happens Here

No spoilers — here is what matters:

The <Glossary>straw hat pirates</Glossary> arrive
at a city built on water. Something goes wrong.
Everything falls apart. The crew has to decide
what they are actually willing to fight for.

Then they fight for it.

## Key Episodes

**Episode 207** — Water 7 begins.
The city is beautiful. Something feels off.

**Episode 227** — Things start unraveling.
This is where the emotional gut punches begin.

**Episode 264** — Enies Lobby begins.
One of the greatest arcs in anime history starts here.
Buckle up.

**Episode 278** — The moment.
You will know exactly what we mean
when you get here.

**Episode 312** — The conclusion of Enies Lobby.
Fans consider this one of the greatest
episode sequences in One Piece history.

## Featured Music

**Kokoro no Chizu (Map of the Heart)** —
The opening theme during Water 7.
Perfectly captures the emotional weight
of what you are about to experience.

**Crazy Rainbow** — Arrives during
Enies Lobby. Energetic and hopeful
in exactly the right moments.

**Yume wa Owaranai** — The ending theme.
Quietly devastating after certain episodes.
