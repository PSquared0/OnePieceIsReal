The <Glossary>Fishman Island Saga</Glossary> is
your first look at the post-<Glossary>timeskip</Glossary>
<Glossary>straw hat pirates</Glossary>.

Two years have passed. The crew trained.
The world moved on. Now everyone reunites
and sets sail for the <Glossary>new world</Glossary>.

This saga is lighter than what came before it.
Intentionally so. After Marineford you need
to breathe. Oda knew that.

Enjoy the reunion. Enjoy the color.
Enjoy seeing how much everyone has grown.

The <Glossary>new world</Glossary> is waiting.

## What Happens Here

No spoilers — here is what matters:

The crew enters the ocean floor.
A kingdom of merfolk exists beneath the sea.
History and prejudice run deep here.

The <Glossary>straw hat pirates</Glossary>
arrive and immediately cause a scene.

Some things never change. 🏴‍☠️

## Key Episodes

**Episode 517** — The crew reunites.
After everything that happened this moment
lands harder than you expect.

**Episode 523** — Fishman Island begins properly.
The new world aesthetic kicks in immediately.

**Episode 541** — The arc finds its emotional core.
A history lesson that recontextualizes
everything you thought you knew.

**Episode 573** — The conclusion.
A satisfying end to the first Saga
of the second half.
