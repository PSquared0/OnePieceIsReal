The <Glossary>Egghead Saga</Glossary> is the
opening chapter of the Final Saga.

If Wano was One Piece at its emotional peak
then Egghead is One Piece at its most
intellectually ambitious.

Oda starts answering questions that fans
have been asking for 20 years. The lore
of the world deepens dramatically.
The stakes reach a scale the series
has never attempted before.

And Toei matched every moment of it with
animation that surpasses even Wano.

The final saga has begun. 🏴‍☠️

## What Happens Here

No spoilers — here is what matters:

The crew arrives at a research island
unlike anything they have seen before.

The person who lives there knows things
about the world that the <Glossary>world
government</Glossary> has been hiding
for 800 years.

They want that person silenced.

The <Glossary>straw hat pirates</Glossary>
are in the way.

## Key Episodes

**Episode 1086** — Egghead begins.
The animation quality is immediately
staggering. You will notice within minutes.

**Episode 1093** — The situation escalates.
This is where you realize Egghead is
playing in a completely different league.

**Episode 1100** — Major revelations begin.
20 year old mysteries start getting answers.

**Episode 1110** — One of the greatest
episodes in the entire series.
The animation alone is worth the journey.

**Episode 1155** — The conclusion.
The last episode before the Elbaf arc begins.
The world of One Piece will never
be the same.

## Episodes To Skip

None.

Every episode of Egghead is
<Glossary>manga canon</Glossary>.

Toei eliminated filler entirely for
the Final Saga. Every episode counts.

## Featured Music

**Dreamy-Go-Round** — The opening theme
for Egghead. Whimsical on the surface.
Carries an undercurrent of something
much heavier underneath.

**Raise** — Arrives mid arc.
More urgent and driving.
Perfectly captures the escalating stakes.

**New Genesis** — The ending theme.
One of the most beautiful pieces
of music in the entire series.
