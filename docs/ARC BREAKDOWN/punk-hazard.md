Punk Hazard is One Piece's table-setting arc
for the second half of the series.

It introduces characters, factions, and plot
threads that will matter for hundreds of
episodes to come. It is not the most beloved
arc but it is one of the most important.

New viewers sometimes find it dense.
That is because it IS dense. Oda is
building something enormous here and
Punk Hazard is laying the foundation.

Pay attention. Everything matters.

## What Happens Here

No spoilers — here is what matters:

The crew receives a distress call from
a government facility on a dangerous island.
Half the island is on fire. Half is frozen solid.

They investigate anyway. Obviously.

New alliances form. New enemies appear.
The <Glossary>new world</Glossary> starts
showing its teeth.

## Key Episodes

**Episode 579** — Punk Hazard begins.
The island immediately establishes
that the new world plays by different rules.

**Episode 591** — The situation
becomes significantly more complicated.
Classic One Piece escalation begins.

**Episode 609** — A major alliance forms.
This changes the direction of the
story going forward in a big way.

**Episode 624** — Punk Hazard concludes.
Set up complete. Dressrosa is next.
The real game begins.
