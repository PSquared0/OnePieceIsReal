The <Glossary>Marineford Saga</Glossary> is the
turning point of the entire series.

Everything One Piece has been building toward
since Episode 1 converges here. The <Glossary>yonko</Glossary>.
The <Glossary>marines</Glossary>. The <Glossary>seven
warlords of the sea</Glossary>. The <Glossary>world
government</Glossary>. All of it.

This saga broke the One Piece fandom when it aired.
It still does.

## What Happens Here

No spoilers — here is what matters:

The world goes to war.

Luffy runs straight into the middle of it.

## Key Episodes

**Episode 326** — Thriller Bark begins
after the filler. A haunted ship arc that
provides crucial character development
before the storm ahead.

**Episode 385** — Sabaody Archipelago begins.
The tone shifts dramatically.
Pay close attention to everything here.

**Episode 408** — The war begins.
This is one of the most ambitious
arcs in anime history.

**Episode 483 to 489** — The conclusion
of Marineford.

## Featured Music

**Share the World** — The opening theme
that carries you through the buildup.
Hopeful in a way that feels almost cruel
in hindsight.

**One Day** — Arrives at exactly the right
moment. One of the most emotionally
resonant openings in the series.

**Kaze wo Sagashite** — The ending theme
during the darkest stretch of this saga.
Very intentional choice by Toei.
