The <Glossary>Skypiea Saga</Glossary> is One Piece's
most divisive arc among new viewers.

It will feel different. The setting is different.
The stakes feel disconnected from everything else.
New viewers often consider skipping it.

Do not skip it.

Skypiea contains some of the most important
thematic groundwork in the entire series.
Things introduced here pay off hundreds of
episodes later. Oda planned this from the start.

Trust the journey. 🏴‍☠️

## What Happens Here

No spoilers — here is what matters:

The <Glossary>straw hat pirates</Glossary> sail
into the sky. An island exists above the clouds.
A war has been going on for centuries.

The crew has nothing to do with any of this.

They get involved anyway.

## Key Episodes

**Episode 144** — Skypiea begins properly
after the filler. The tone shift is immediate.

**Episode 153** — The adventure finds
its footing. Things start clicking.

**Episode 195** — The conclusion.
One of the most quietly powerful endings
in the entire series.
