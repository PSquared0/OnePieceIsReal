This page covers the life, career, and creative process of the manga artist behind One Piece — essential context for understanding the story's depth and consistency.

**Eiichiro Oda** is the creator, writer, and illustrator of
One Piece. Born on January 1, 1975 in Kumamoto, Japan, Oda
decided at age four that he wanted to be a manga artist
specifically to avoid getting a real job. Respect. 🏴‍☠️

He launched One Piece in **Weekly Shonen Jump** in 1997
at just 22 years old. Nearly 30 years later he is still
writing it.

## His Family

Oda met his wife **Chiaki Inaba** in 2002 at Jump Festa
where she was cosplaying as **Nami** for a One Piece
stage show. Yes, he met his wife because she was
cosplaying his own character. Yes, she looks exactly
like Nami in real life. The story writes itself. 💕

They married in November 2004 and have two daughters together.

Oda keeps his family life very private. He actually lives
at his workplace most of the time and visits his family
on vacations while his wife and kids visit him once a week.

The dedication to One Piece is real.

## Fun Facts

* He decided to become a manga artist at **age four**
  to avoid a real job
* His biggest influence was **Akira Toriyama** and Dragon Ball
* He reportedly sleeps only **3 hours a night** working
  from 5am to 2am
* He planned the **ending of One Piece before writing
  the first chapter**
* He donated **800 million yen** to his hometown Kumamoto
  after a devastating earthquake in 2016, one donation
  made under **Luffy's name**
* He personally supervised the **Netflix live action**
  series to protect the source material
* He holds a **Guinness World Record** for most copies
  published in the same comic series by a single author
* Fans voted him into his own character popularity polls
  even though he never officially put himself in the manga

## The Fan Theory Worth Knowing

A mysterious account called **Sandman** on X is beloved
in the <Glossary>One Piece</Glossary> community for sharing rare photos and
translations of Oda's SBS columns. Fans have long
speculated about who runs it given the access to photos
almost nobody else has. Nobody knows for sure.

The mystery is very on brand for One Piece. 🏴‍☠️
