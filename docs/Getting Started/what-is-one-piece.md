One Piece is a Japanese manga and anime series created by
**Eiichiro Oda** in 1997. It is the best selling manga of
all time with over **500 million copies** in circulation worldwide.

## The Story

It follows **Monkey D. Luffy**, a boy who accidentally ate a
<Glossary>devil fruit</Glossary> that gave his body the
properties of rubber.
His dream? Become <Glossary>pirate king</Glossary>.

The story takes place across vast oceans, mysterious islands,
and a world full of pirates, marines, and people chasing freedom.

The legendary treasure known as **the <Glossary>one piece</Glossary>**
was left behind by the previous <Glossary>pirate king</Glossary>,
**Gol D. Roger.**
Nobody knows what it is. Everyone wants it.

## Why Do People Love It?

* **World building** — one of the most detailed fictional
  worlds ever created
* **Characters** you will fall in love with
* **Emotional payoff** — arcs that make you laugh AND cry
* **Themes** of freedom, justice, found family, and courage
